# Inkme Nav — a directory of legal ebook resources

[English](README.md) · [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

**Live site: [nav.e-ink.me](https://nav.e-ink.me)** (English) · [nav.e-ink.me/zh](https://nav.e-ink.me/zh) (中文)

A hand-picked, bilingual directory of places to get ebooks legally: free libraries, stores, library borrowing, reading apps, tools, audiobooks, communities, and dictionaries — with an eye toward what actually works on an e-ink reader. Every listing says what the site offers, in which formats, at what price, and whether it needs an account.

Every entry is legal for a stated reason: the text is in the public domain, the rights holder released it, a public institution lends it, or a licensed store sells it. Pirate and shadow libraries are not listed and never will be.

The eight shelves below mirror the site. Each table is the short version; the site adds essays, per-entry detail pages, and filters by format, language, and price.

## [Free ebooks](https://nav.e-ink.me/free-ebooks)

A free ebook is not the same thing as a file someone uploaded without permission. Everything on this shelf is free because the text is in the public domain, the rights holder released it, or a publisher opened a scholarly edition.

| Site | What it offers | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org) | The oldest digital library: tens of thousands of public-domain books in EPUB, Kindle, PDF, and plain text | Free |
| [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) | English public-domain classics redone properly — corrected texts, modern typography, new covers | Free |
| [Internet Archive](https://archive.org) | Millions of scanned volumes; public-domain items download freely, in-copyright items lend one reader at a time | Free |
| [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) | A catalog page for every book, plus one-copy-one-reader ebook lending | Free |
| [ManyBooks](https://manybooks.net) | A friendlier browsing face over the same public-domain pile | Free (paid extras) |
| [Wikisource](https://wikisource.org) | Volunteer-proofread source texts in dozens of language editions | Free |
| [DOAB](https://www.doabooks.org) | Directory of peer-reviewed academic books that publishers have chosen to open | Free |
| [OAPEN](https://library.oapen.org) | Hosts the open-access files themselves, with a strong European list | Free |
| [HathiTrust](https://www.hathitrust.org) | Research-institution preservation library; full downloads for public-domain works | Free |
| [OpenStax](https://openstax.org) | Complete peer-reviewed textbooks, readable on the web or as PDF | Free |
| [Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org) | Pre-modern Chinese writing: originals, transcriptions, and some English translation | Free |
| [书格 Shuge](https://www.shuge.org) | High-resolution scans of public-domain rare books and prints | Free |

If a modern bestseller is missing from this shelf, that is the law, not an oversight. The legal next step is a library loan or a store — both below.

## [Ebook stores](https://nav.e-ink.me/ebook-stores)

For books still in copyright. What you buy is usually a license inside one ecosystem, not a file you can pass around — the practical question is which ecosystem you are already in, and which format will still open on the device you read with.

| Site | What it offers | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Amazon Kindle Store](https://www.amazon.com/kindle-store) | The largest English shop; Kindle formats (KFX, AZW3) tied to Kindle hardware and apps | Paid |
| [Kobo Store](https://www.kobo.com) | EPUB and Kobo's KEPUB — the native shop for Kobo readers, with an optional Kobo Plus subscription | Paid |
| [Google Play Books](https://play.google.com/store/books) | Ebooks and audiobooks on your Google account, plus a 1,000-file personal upload shelf | Paid |
| [Apple Books](https://books.apple.com) | Built into every iPhone, iPad, and Mac; EPUB behind FairPlay DRM | Paid |
| [微信读书 WeRead](https://weread.qq.com) | Deep Chinese catalog on a WeChat account: free reading with time limits, membership on top | Free + paid |
| [豆瓣阅读 Douban Read](https://read.douban.com) | Pay-per-title Chinese ebooks and magazines, with a strong line in original writing | Paid |
| [京东读书 JD Read](https://ebooks.jd.com) | JD.com's ebook shelf mixing free titles, paid books, and a membership | Free + paid |

Catalogs, prices, and even which storefront opens follow the country of your account — a title on the US Kindle store is not a promise about the German or Japanese one. The Chinese shops assume a reader in mainland China.

## [Library borrowing](https://nav.e-ink.me/library-borrowing)

The legal middle path: the book is in copyright, you do not want to buy it, and a public institution has already paid the license. You need a card, not a workaround.

| Site | What it offers | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Libby](https://libbyapp.com) | Borrow ebooks and audiobooks with a public-library card (US, Canada, UK, Australia, NZ) | Free with a library card |
| [Hoopla](https://www.hoopladigital.com) | Library lending without waitlists — monthly borrow caps instead (US and Canada) | Free with a library card |
| [中国国家图书馆 NLC](https://www.nlc.cn) | Digitized rare books, modern ebooks, and reader-portal lending from China's national library | Free with a reader card |

Holds, loan length, and how many books you may have out are set by your library. A popular new novel can have a longer queue than buying it — that is still the point of the service.

## [Reading apps](https://nav.e-ink.me/reading-apps)

The program that opens a file you already have — as distinct from the store window onto books you pay for. Mixing those two jobs up is how people end up buying a second copy of a book they sideloaded last year.

| Site | What it offers | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [KOReader](https://koreader.rocks) | Open-source reader built for buttons, ghosting, and long sessions; opens nearly every format | Free |
| [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com) | The desktop half of the setup: library database, format converter, device transfer | Free |
| [Moon+ Reader 静读天下](https://www.moondownload.com) | The old Android workhorse for files on a memory card | Free + paid |
| [ReadEra](https://readera.org) | Quiet Android and iOS reader, no account required | Free + paid |
| [FBReader](https://fbreader.org) | Veteran reader from the open-source FB2 era, now with a premium tier | Free + paid |
| [Legado 开源阅读](https://github.com/gedoor/legado) | Open-source Chinese Android reader for local files and sources you configure yourself | Free |
| [Thorium Reader](https://www.edrlab.org/software/thorium-reader/) | Desktop EPUB/PDF/DAISY reader from EDRLab; also opens library LCP loans | Free |
| [Kindle App](https://www.amazon.com/kindle-dbs/fd/kcp) | The Kindle store off the hardware — reads store formats and personal documents | Free |
| [Kobo App](https://www.kobo.com) | The Kobo store off the hardware | Free |
| [微信读书 App](https://weread.qq.com) | WeRead on the phone; also opens local EPUB, PDF, TXT, MOBI, AZW3 | Free + paid |

Sideloading fails for one reason most of the time: the file is not in a format the reader likes. Convert first, then copy.

## [Ebook tools](https://nav.e-ink.me/ebook-tools)

The unglamorous shelf that decides whether a legal file is actually pleasant to read. Two of these are part of this site's family; they get a short entry rather than a speech.

| Site | What it offers | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Inkme](https://e-ink.me) | Web page to EPUB, plus conversions among MOBI, PDF, EPUB, HTML, and TXT *(this family)* | Free credits, then paid |
| [iloveepub.com](https://iloveepub.com) | In-browser EPUB utilities — compress, merge, split, convert; files stay on your machine *(this family)* | Free |
| [Sigil](https://sigil-ebook.com) | The open-source EPUB editor for changing the book, not just rewrapping it | Free |
| [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org) | Command-line converter from Markdown, DOCX, HTML into EPUB and PDF | Free |

A tool does not launder a file: converting a book you have no right to read does not create a right to read it.

## [Audiobooks](https://nav.e-ink.me/audiobooks)

A performance with its own copyright, even when the underlying book is old. That is why a free text on Gutenberg is not automatically a free recording.

| Site | What it offers | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LibriVox](https://librivox.org) | Volunteer-read public-domain audiobooks, free to keep, mostly MP3 and M4B | Free |
| [Audible](https://www.audible.com) | Amazon's credit-based subscription and store — the deepest commercial English shelf | Subscription |
| [喜马拉雅 Ximalaya](https://www.ximalaya.com) | The large Chinese audio service: audiobooks, lectures, podcasts | Free + paid |
| [Storytel](https://www.storytel.com) | Streaming-first subscription, strong in the Nordic countries | Subscription |

Libraries lend commercial audiobooks too (see Libby and Hoopla above) — often the better price for a book you will hear once.

## About this repository

This repository holds the source of the site. The entire catalog — every listing above, in both languages — is one data file, [`src/data/sites.yaml`](src/data/sites.yaml); the site's pages are generated from it. The design spec lives in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).
